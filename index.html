How to Play

Objective

Guide the coral player from the top-left corner to the lime exit at the bottom-right.

Controls

  • Arrow keys or WASD — move
  • Swipe — move on touch devices
  • P — pause / resume
  • H — hint (costs points)
  • R — new maze

Scoring

  • Base score 100, reduced by time, moves, and hints
  • Higher difficulty multiplies your score
  • Compete on the global leaderboard

Difficulty

  • Easy — small maze, cheap hints
  • Medium — balanced
  • Hard — large maze
  • Expert — the ultimate test
